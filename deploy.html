Deploy Buildbond

Two contracts on Robinhood Chain from your wallet: the vault implementation, then the factory that clones it. The deploying wallet becomes the guardian: it names $BOND once and can change the builder only through a 48 hour timelock. It has no power over any vault's money.

  1. Make a fresh wallet for the keeper. Its key goes into Vercel as BUILDBOND_KEEPER_KEY and nowhere else; give it a little ETH for gas. Its address is the builder below.
  2. Deploy the vault implementation, then the factory with the builder address.
  3. Put the factory into config.js and push. Then turn on the builder workflow's schedule.
  4. When $BOND is live on Pons, name it here so the burns can start.
